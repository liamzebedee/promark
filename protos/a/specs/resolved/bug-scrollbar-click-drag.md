# Bug: Verify Scrollbar Click and Drag Works

## Reproduction Steps

1. Open a document longer than the viewport
2. Click on the scrollbar thumb
3. Drag the scrollbar up and down
4. Also try: click on the scrollbar track (above/below thumb) to jump

## Expected Behavior

- Clicking and dragging the scrollbar thumb should scroll the document smoothly
- Clicking on the track should jump the view to that position
- Scrollbar should feel responsive and match standard OS scrollbar behavior

## Actual Behavior

To be verified - user suspects scrollbar interaction may not be working.

## Severity

Medium - Scrollbar is a core navigation mechanism for long documents.
